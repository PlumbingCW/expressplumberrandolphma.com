How to choose the right service

Most plumbing problems fall into a few groups. Water that will not stop, sewage rising, no hot water and no heat are emergencies. Slow drains, dripping fixtures and old equipment are best handled soon but do not usually need a midnight call.

If water is flooding the house, start with burst pipe repair and shut off the main valve. If a drain is backing up, drain cleaning is the right page, and a whole-house backup belongs under sewer line cleaning. For a dead or leaking tank, see water heater replacement.

Emergency services

These services are dispatched day and night, including weekends and holidays. A plumber is sent as soon as one is free, and active flooding and sewage backups come first.

Planned services

Installation, repiping and waterproofing are scheduled with you. Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows.

Licensed plumbers and permits

Plumbing and gas fitting in Massachusetts must be done by a plumber or gas fitter licensed by the Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters. Permits are required before plumbing is installed, altered, removed, replaced or repaired, and only licensed plumbers can pull them. Minor repairs, such as a leaky faucet or a blocked drain, do not need a permit.

Randolph's building department handles permits at 41 South Main Street, and the plumbing inspector is in on Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. You can confirm any plumber's license through the state's eLIPSE portal.
