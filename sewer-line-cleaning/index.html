When the whole house backs up at once

Branch clogs affect one fixture. The main building drain serves every fixture, so when it clogs the lowest drain in the house shows it first, usually a basement floor drain or a shower on the ground floor.

That is why a backup that begins when the washer or dishwasher drains is a main line problem. The water has nowhere to go but up. Stop using every fixture, because each flush or load sends more water into the clog.

If one sink alone is slow, drain cleaning is the right page. If the whole house is affected, you are in the right place.

How a main line cleaning works

Find the cleanout

A cleanout is a capped pipe, usually near where the line leaves the house. If none exists, we install one.

Run the cable

A larger cable with a cutting head works from the cleanout toward the street.

Check the flow

We run water to confirm the line drains freely and stays that way.

Camera the line

A short camera run shows roots, grease, offsets or cracks left behind.

What usually blocks a main line

Tree roots

Fine roots enter a joint and grow into a mass that catches paper and grease.

Grease buildup

Kitchen grease hardens in the line and narrows it over years.

Wipes and sanitary products

Even products labeled flushable catch on roots and rough pipe walls.

A belly in the line

A low sag holds water and debris. Cleaning helps but does not cure it.

Collapsed or offset pipe

A cable stops at the break. Repair is the only fix, as covered on our sewer line repair page.
