Emergency toilet repair: stop the overflow first

Stop the fill

Remove the tank lid and lift the float, or close the supply valve at the wall.

Do not flush again

Each flush sends more water into the bowl.

Contain the water

Towels at the base help. If water is on the floor above a ceiling, call right away.

Try a flange plunger

A flange plunger seals the bowl outlet. A flat cup plunger will not.

Call if it does not clear

A closet auger or a toilet pull may be needed if an object is stuck.

Why a toilet runs, leaks or will not flush

Flapper does not seal

Water trickles from tank to bowl and the fill valve restarts every few minutes. A new flapper is a common fix.

Fill valve fails

The tank overfills or refills slowly, and water pours into the overflow tube.

Wax ring fails

Water around the base of the toilet or a smell of sewage means the seal under the toilet has failed.

Cracked bowl or tank

Hairline cracks seep water. The toilet needs replacing.

Weak flush

Clogged rim jets or a partial clog in the trap arm reduce the flush.

Wobble at the floor

Loose bolts or a rotted flange let the toilet rock, which breaks the wax seal.

Toilets in Randolph's older houses

Randolph's housing runs from farmhouses and side-hall cottages to postwar Capes and ranches. Older houses often have a single bathroom, so a toilet that will not flush is more than a nuisance.

Floors in older bathrooms can be soft around the flange. If the toilet rocks, the subfloor may have been damp for years. We check the flange and the floor before setting a new toilet, because a new wax ring on a rotten floor will fail again.

Randolph's water is soft, so mineral buildup in the rim jets is less common than in hard-water towns. A weak flush here more often means a partial clog or a worn flapper.

The code behind the work

Toilet and fill valve work follows the standards in 248 CMR 10.00, including the anti-siphon rules that keep tank water out of the supply. A licensed plumber pulls the permit, and the inspector signs off on the finished job.
