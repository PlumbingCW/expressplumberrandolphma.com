Garbage Disposal Installation Cost in Randolph, MA: Factors

By the %s team. Published November 13, 2026. Updated November 13, 2026.

A garbage disposal is a simple appliance, but the price of putting one in depends on the unit and the kitchen it goes into. A like-for-like swap under a sink that already has one is a short job. A first-time installation in a sink with no outlet or switch is a bigger one.

This guide lays out what drives the price so you can compare quotes. We do not post dollar amounts on a web page, because the real cost depends on your sink and your wiring.

Replacement or first-time installation

If you already have a disposal, swapping it is the simpler case. The mounting flange, the drain connections and the electrical supply are in place, and the plumber removes the old unit and fits the new one, often within an hour or two.

A first-time installation is different. The sink needs a disposal flange, the drain may need a different trap and a dishwasher connection, and the sink base needs an outlet or a hard-wired connection and a switch. Each of those adds time and may add a trade.

Wiring

A disposal runs on a standard household circuit. A plug-in model needs an outlet under the sink, often switched. A hard-wired model needs an electrician for the connection.

Drain layout

A double-bowl sink needs a continuous waste arrangement. A single bowl is simpler. If the existing trap is corroded, we replace it at the same visit.

Light duty or heavy duty

Light-duty disposal

  • Smaller motor, lower price.
  • Fine for one or two people and soft scraps.
  • Louder in most models.
  • May jam with tougher waste.

Heavy-duty disposal

  • Larger motor and more grinding power.
  • Better for families and heavy use.
  • Often insulated for quieter running.
  • Higher price and more weight on the sink mount.

Ask your plumber how much you cook and what goes into the sink. A bigger motor is not always better in a small kitchen. Our garbage disposal repair and installation page explains what we handle.

Permits and wiring rules

A direct swap of a disposal is generally a fixture-level job. If the plumbing is changed, such as moving the drain or adding a new waste line, it becomes permitted plumbing work in Massachusetts, and only a licensed plumber can pull the permit. Hard-wired electrical work should be done by a licensed electrician.

We tell you which part of the job falls where before we start. If the sink drain is old, we recommend replacing the trap during the installation, since it is easier and cheaper than doing it later.

Randolph kitchens and drains

Randolph's older houses often have a kitchen drain that runs through a small branch line, and that line can hold years of grease. A new disposal on top of a partly blocked drain can make the sink drain worse, not better.

Randolph's soft water, at 39.6 ppm in the 2024 report, means mineral scale is not a main cause of kitchen clogs here. Grease and food residue are. If your kitchen drain is slow, a cable or hydro jetting before the installation is a wise step.

Questions to ask any plumber before you approve

  • Is the price for the unit and labor, and what is not included? Electrical work and drain repairs are often separate.
  • Will you replace the trap and the drain connections? A corroded trap is better replaced now.
  • Do you haul away the old unit? Ask whether disposal of the old unit is included.
  • Is there a warranty on the unit and the labor? Ask for the terms in writing.
  • Who handles the electrical connection? A hard-wired unit may need an electrician.

How to get the most from a new disposal

A disposal lasts longer with a few habits. Run cold water before, during and after grinding. Feed scraps in small amounts rather than a full load at once. Avoid fibrous and starchy foods, such as celery stalks, potato peels and pasta, which can wrap or paste up in the chamber.

Once a month, grind a handful of ice cubes and a few citrus peels to clean the chamber and freshen it. Do not use a disposal as a trash can. Bones, fruit pits, grease and coffee grounds in quantity belong in the trash or compost.

A disposal that hums but does not spin is jammed, not broken, in most cases. Turn it off at the wall switch and use the hex slot on the bottom, if it has one, to free it. Never put your hand into the unit.
