Sizing a water heater for the household

A tank is sized by how much hot water the household uses in its busiest hour. A family of four with two showers running may need a 50 gallon unit, while a couple may do fine with 40. Recovery rate matters as much as the gallons.

We ask about showers, tubs, laundry and dishwashers and match the unit to the use. Oversized tanks waste fuel and undersized ones run out.

Tankless sizing

Tankless units are sized by flow rate and temperature rise. A cold New England incoming water temperature affects how much a unit can deliver, which is why a gas tankless often needs a larger gas line.

Heat pump water heaters

These need open space and a drain for condensate, and they cool the room they sit in.

Permits and inspections in Randolph

Massachusetts requires a permit before plumbing is installed, altered, removed, replaced or repaired. Only a licensed plumber can pull it. Randolph's plumbing inspector is in on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday, and calls to schedule an inspection go in before 9 a.m.

Keep the permit with your warranty paperwork. If you sell the house, a permitted and inspected installation is a point in your favor.

Permits, code and the old tank

A water heater swap is not a casual job. In Massachusetts it needs a permit, and a gas unit also needs a proper vent, a sediment trap and a code-compliant connection. The local inspector checks the finished work, and in Randolph the Building Department is at 41 S Main St, with the plumbing inspector working Monday, Wednesday and Thursday.

We also take the old tank away. A tank that is full of sediment is heavy and awkward, and many homeowners do not want to deal with disposal. Haul-away is part of the job, so you are not left with it in the basement.
