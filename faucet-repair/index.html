Stop a faucet leak now

Look under the sink for the two small valves, hot on the left and cold on the right. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If water still flows, the problem is a supply line or valve, and the main shutoff is the next step.

A faucet that will not shut off at all is a valve or cartridge failure. A water supply line that is bulging or wet at the braided cover should be replaced soon. A burst supply line can spill gallons per minute, so close the valve at the first sign.

If water is running behind a wall, the issue goes beyond a faucet. The leak detection page explains how we find it.

Common faucet problems and the fix

Drip from the spout

Worn washer, seat or cartridge. The part is replaced and the faucet tested.

Leak around the handle

A worn O-ring or packing nut. Often a quick repair.

Low flow or poor pressure

A clogged aerator or a failing cartridge.

Stuck or stiff handle

Mineral buildup or a corroded cartridge. Randolph's soft water makes this less common, but it does happen.

Water under the sink

A loose supply line, a bad shutoff or a leaking drain trap.

Shower diverter fails

The diverter inside the tub spout or valve does not switch, and water sprays from both places.

Repair the faucet or replace it?

Repair when

  • The faucet body is sound.
  • A single part has worn out.
  • Parts are available for your brand.
  • The finish is in good shape.

Replace when

  • The body is cracked or heavily corroded.
  • Parts are discontinued.
  • The faucet is more than 15 years old.
  • You want a different style or height.

Many kitchen and bathroom faucet jobs also involve a leaking trap or a dull shutoff. We replace those at the same visit and check for leaks.

Faucets and the state permit rule

The Massachusetts plumbing consumer fact sheet says repairing a leaky faucet is a minor repair and does not need a permit. Replacing or relocating a faucet, a valve or the piping behind it does not count as minor, and a licensed plumber pulls the permit.

That distinction is why we tell you at the start if a faucet repair will turn into a valve or pipe replacement. A new angle stop under the sink is quick, and a new line behind the wall is a separate quote.

Randolph homes and faucet leaks

Older Randolph kitchens and bathrooms often have shutoff valves that have not been turned in decades. An old valve can seize or leak when it is first closed. We carry replacement valves so a seized one does not leave you without water.

If your bathtub faucet or shower valve drips inside the wall, the repair requires access from the back, usually through a closet or an opened wall. The plumbing installation page covers new valves during a remodel.
