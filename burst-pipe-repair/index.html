Why pipes burst in Randolph

Winter freezes are the usual cause, and the pipes most at risk run along exterior walls, through crawlspaces, in garages and under porches. Ice swells inside the copper or PEX, and the split often shows only after the thaw.

Freezing is not the only cause. Randolph homes built in the mid-century boom can have copper lines that have corroded thin, and pressure surges from a failing regulator can finish them off. Older houses near the town center may still have galvanized steel inside walls, which rusts from the inside until a pinhole opens up.

Frozen pipe, not yet burst

If a faucet runs a trickle or not at all in cold weather, the pipe may be frozen but intact. Keep the tap open, warm the area gently with a hair dryer and call. Never use an open flame.

Splits that appear after the thaw

A pipe that froze and cracked can start leaking only when the ice melts. Check the basement and walls after a cold snap even if the water seems fine.

Water damage after the pipe is fixed

A plumber's job ends at the pipe. Drywall, insulation and flooring that stayed wet for more than a day or two need drying by a restoration crew, or mold can start inside the wall.

We note where the water traveled and give you a written summary of the repair that you can share with your insurer. We do not diagnose mold, and we do not promise insurance outcomes, but a quick report helps the claim.

If the burst is in the supply line from the street, the repair belongs to the property owner from the property line to the house. The water service line repair page explains what that involves, and a leak detection visit finds a slow leak that has not yet burst.

Stop the next freeze before it starts

  • Let faucets on exterior walls drip during a hard freeze.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on cold nights so heat reaches the pipes.
  • Disconnect outdoor hoses and shut off the supply to outdoor spigots in the fall.
  • Insulate pipes in the garage, crawlspace and under porches.
  • Know where your main shutoff is and test that it turns.

What to do before the plumber arrives

Shut off the water at the main valve first. Do not wait to find the break. Then open a cold tap on the lowest floor to drain the line, and switch off the water heater so it does not run dry.

Stay away from any water that is near outlets, the electrical panel or appliances, and do not touch them. Move rugs and small furniture out of the wet area, and take photos of the damage before you clean up, since your insurer will want them. Repairs in Massachusetts follow 248 CMR 10.00, the Uniform State Plumbing Code, and a licensed plumber pulls any permit.
