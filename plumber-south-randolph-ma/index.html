Where South Randolph fits in Randolph's history

Randolph began as the South Precinct of Braintree and was incorporated as its own town in 1793, according to the town's new resident page. The name South Randolph is a later, informal label, and it appears in neighborhood directories and real estate guides, not in the town's planning documents.

That is worth stating plainly. We found no official boundary for South Randolph, and no town source that defines it. Directories describe it as a small suburban area with quiet streets. When we take a call, we place you by your street and your nearest cross street instead of by a line on a map.

For any problem that involves the town's pipes, the Randolph DPW is at 781-961-0940, with water on 781-961-0942 and sewer on 781-961-0941.

Houses, apartments and who is responsible

Neighborhood data for the area describes small to medium single-family houses next to apartment complexes, and a mixture of owners and renters. It also says many homes were built between 1940 and 1969, with some from later decades. These are summary statistics for a census tract, and they do not tell us what material your pipes are.

The mix of owners and renters changes who you call first. In a rental, a leak or a failed water heater is usually a message for the landlord or property manager. If you own the home, or you are the manager and need a plumber, the problem is yours to solve, and we can help you decide how urgent it is.

In a building with several units, a backup can start in a shared line. If more than one unit is affected, tell the manager before anyone pours a chemical cleaner down a drain.

Common calls in South Randolph, and what to do first

  • A toilet that will not stop running. Lift the tank lid and check the fill valve and flapper. If water keeps running, close the valve behind the toilet and call. See toilet repair.
  • A slow kitchen or bath drain. Remove the stopper and clear visible hair or food. If it returns within days, the blockage is farther down the pipe.
  • A water heater that leaks. Close the cold water valve above it, switch off the power or gas control, and call.
  • A drip under a sink. Place a bucket, close the stop valve under the sink and call. A leaking supply line is a quick repair.

Water facts that apply across Randolph

Randolph's water report lists hardness of 39.6 ppm, on the soft side, so scale is a smaller issue here than in many towns. Soft water can be somewhat harsher on old metal fittings, and the same report notes that one of thirty sampled homes was above the lead action level. If you do not know what your service line or interior pipes are made of, ask for it to be checked.

Larger work needs a permit from the Building Department at 41 S Main St, and the plumbing inspector is in on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday. Calls to that office go in before 9am.
