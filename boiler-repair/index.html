Boiler won't heat: check these first

  • Thermostat and power. Set the thermostat well above room temperature and check the boiler switch and its breaker.
  • Pressure gauge. A cold hot-water boiler normally sits around 12 to 15 psi. Zero means a leak or a bad fill valve.
  • Water level on steam boilers. The sight glass should be about half full. A low level shuts the boiler off.
  • Reset button. One press resets a locked-out burner. If it trips again, stop and call.
  • Fuel supply. A gas valve open, an oil tank with fuel and a clean filter all matter. Do not try to relight a gas boiler with a smell of gas.

Boiler plumbing is our scope. Oil burner service is handled by the licensed oil heat technician, and we tell you when that is the right call.

Common boiler faults

Cold radiators

Trapped air in baseboards or radiators. Bleeding the air restores heat.

Low pressure

A leak in the system, a failed expansion tank or a bad fill valve.

Banging or kettling

Scale or sediment on the heat exchanger, or a pump issue.

Leaks at the boiler

A failed relief valve, a loose fitting or a cracked section. Some are repairs and some mean replacement.

Short cycling

The boiler starts and stops quickly. Oversizing, a failing thermostat or a dirty flame sensor can do it.

No domestic hot water

On a combi or indirect system, the boiler or the tank loop may be failing.
